RoboQuest: example task videos

Anonymous supplementary page for the review of RoboQuest. The same videos are in the supplementary material.

One scripted demonstration per task, taken from the released demonstration dataset. The scripts know the hidden information but explore as an agent without it would, so each video shows the searching, inspection or testing the task requires.

Layout. Top: the task, its family and the goal text given to the agent. Middle: the three camera views the agents receive (left scene camera, right scene camera, wrist camera). Bottom: the current stage of the demonstration, from the stage annotation stored with every frame; Puzzle Box also shows the outcome of each attempt. All videos play at 3× speed, 20 frames per second.

1. Puzzle BoxTest

A chain of sliding bolts locks the lid, and a bolt moves only when nothing blocks it. In this episode three attempts fail before the robot finds an order that opens the box.

2. Stamp CompositionTest

The stamp patterns are hidden inside opaque housings. The robot prints each stamp on a scratch board to learn its pattern, then composes the reference pattern on the final board.

3. Wobbly StandTest

A ball released on the stand rolls toward the short leg. The robot shims that leg, tests again with the ball, and switches to a thicker shim when the stand is still uneven.

4. Odd ParcelTest

The odd parcels look like the others and are found by weighing parcels against each other on a balance.

5. Painted CubesInspect

The deciding marks are on faces the cameras cannot see at the start. The robot turns each cube to read it, then sorts the cubes into two bins.

6. Marked MugsInspect

Each vessel carries a label underneath. The robot lifts and tilts each one to read its label, then places it on the matching pad.

7. Unfamiliar ContainersInspect

The items are inside boxes with unfamiliar opening mechanisms. The robot tries different actions on each box until it opens.

8. Locked StorageSearch

A compartment opens only while the token of its colour rests on its reader. Tokens may be inside other compartments, so finding one changes where the robot can search next.

9. Search RoomSearch

The targets are hidden in closed compartments around the kitchen. The robot opens compartments until it has found all of them.

10. Blackout SearchSearch

The kitchen is dark. The robot carries a lantern that lights about a metre around itself, and sets it down beside whatever it works on.
